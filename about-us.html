About Gourmet Popcornica

Gourmet Popcornica is the 5th largest producer of popcorn in the world, built with a vision to deliver world-class popcorn experiences through consistency, quality, and scale.

What began as a focused effort to improve popcorn quality in India has evolved into a growing ecosystem serving cinemas, institutional partners, and food businesses across the country.

Today, Gourmet Popcornica stands at the intersection of agriculture, innovation, and large-scale supply, helping shape the future of popcorn in India.

S.B.P. Pattabhi Rama Rao

The Corn Man

A Vision That Changed the Industry

S.B.P. Pattabhi Rama Rao, Managing Director of Gourmet Popcornica Pvt. Ltd., is pioneering the complete popcorn value chain in India. Since incorporating the company in 2014, he has built a farmer-centric powerhouse connecting cultivation and processing to retail and cinemas.

At a time when India depended heavily on imports, he envisioned a self-reliant domestic popcorn ecosystem. Under his leadership, Gourmet Popcornica has grown to deliver international-quality gourmet popcorn to discerning consumers nationwide.

With a stellar track record in food & beverage and retail, he previously spearheaded SPI Cinemas' operations and drove the successful launch and expansion of CookieMan, the iconic Australian cookie brand, across India.

S.B.P. Pattabhi Rama Rao

Managing Director

S.B.P. Pattabhi Rama Rao is pioneering the complete popcorn value chain in India – from hybrid seed cultivation and kernel processing to cinema supplies, retail, and premium branded gourmet popcorn. As an agribusiness leader, he has transformed Gourmet Popcornica into a ₹500+ Crore enterprise, commanding 48% of the country’s popcorn market and empowering over 17,500 farmers through contract farming across eight states.

Areas of Responsibility

  • Value Chain Indigenisation
  • Farmers Partnership & Contract Farming
  • Cinema & Institutional Snacking Solutions
